    Self-check quiz (12 questions)

    Q1. ₹1.8 lakh ke printers ki supply ke liye kaunsa document banega? (Rule 225(iv)(a))

    Rule 225(iv)(a): ₹2.5 lakh tak ki simple purchase ke liye purchase order (with terms & conditions) kaafi hai.

    Q2. Letter of Award ke baad contract kitne din mein execute karna hai? (Rule 225(vi))

    21 days; failure → award annulled and bid security (EMD) forfeited.

    Q3. Price Variation Clause kab allowed hai? (Rule 225(viii)(a))

    PVC ordinarily sirf long-term contracts mein — delivery period exceeding 18 months; short-term mein firm & fixed price.

    Q4. Contract ke liye authority ka source kya hai? (Rule 224(1))

    Rule 224(1) — Article 299(1) of the Constitution; contract 'for and on behalf of the President of India'.

    Q5. DoE OM 03.06.2024 ke anusaar arbitration clause (agar rakha jaye) kis dispute value tak restrict hogi?

    Disputes valued less than ₹10 crore; above that only after reasoned decision approved by Secretary / officer not below JS (MD for CPSE/PSB).

    Q6. Contract close hone ke kitne saal baad koi claim entertain nahi hoga? (Rule 225(xix))

    No claim after 3 years from the date of contract closure (unless contract specifies otherwise).

    Q7. Arbitral award challenge karne par kitna % amount contractor ko (BG ke against) dena hai? (Rule 227A)

    75% of award amount (including interest up to award date) against bank guarantee — DoE OM 29.10.2021.

    Q8. Performance security kab tak valid rehni chahiye? (Rule 171)

    Rule 171: performance security valid for 60 days beyond completion of all contractual obligations including warranty.

    Q9. Supplier ki apni deri par extension diya — LD aur denial clause ka kya hoga?

    Supplier-default extension: formal amendment with LD and denial clause; force-majeure extension: neither.

    Q10. Arbitral award ko court mein challenge karne ki statutory samay-seema? (A&C Act 1996 s.34)

    Section 34(3): three months from receipt of award; court may condone a further 30 days, not thereafter.

    Q11. Typical Manual clause par ₹10 lakh ke goods 20 din late — LD kitna? (0.5%/week or part, max 10%)

    20 days = 3 weeks (part counts) × 0.5% = 1.5% = ₹15,000. Illustrative; actual rate per contract.

    Q12. DoE OM 29.04.2026 (Force Majeure) ke tahat obligations kitne mahine extend ho sakti hain?

    ≥2 and ≤4 months, case-to-case, no cost/penalty, for obligations falling due on/after 28.02.2026; party must not be in default as on 27.02.2026.
