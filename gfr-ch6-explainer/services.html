ProvisionExam trap / granular point
Rule 173Transparency, competition, fairness; self-contained bidding document; NIL charges/consideration ⇒ bid unresponsive; negotiation after bid opening severely discouraged; single bid can still be valid if adequately advertised, criteria not restrictive, prices reasonable (Rule 173(xx)–(xxi)).
Rule 174Efficiency, economy, accountability; delegate purchasing powers to lower functionaries; place contract within original bid validity.
Rule 175Code of Integrity — no bribe/reward/gift, no omission or misrepresentation, no collusion/bid rigging, no improper use of information, no financial transactions between bidder and official, no coercion, no obstruction of investigation, no false declaration; conflict of interest must be disclosed; disclosure of transgressions in the last three years. Rule 175(2): reasonable opportunity of being heard before action.
Rule 176Buy-Back — trade the old item while buying the new one; clause in the bidding document.
Rule 177Consulting Service = project-specific, intellectual, procedural services where deliverables vary from consultant to consultant. Does NOT include direct engagement of a retired Government servant.
Rule 178Hire consultants for a job well defined in terms of content and time frame.
Rule 180Engage consultants only where high-quality services are needed and the Ministry lacks expertise; approval of competent authority BEFORE engaging.
Rule 181 / 182Scope in simple, concise language with eligibility/prequalification criteria; estimate reasonable expenditure from market conditions.
Rule 183Up to ₹50 lakh: long list from formal/informal enquiries. Above ₹50 lakh: EOI to be published on GeM and GeM-CPP (+ own website).
Rule 184Short listed consultants not less than three.
Rule 185TOR: precise objectives · outline of tasks · schedule · Ministry's inputs · final outputs.
Rule 186RFP to shortlisted consultants: LOI, information to consultants, TOR, eligibility criteria, key positions/CVs, evaluation criteria, standard formats, contract terms, mid-term review procedure.
Rule 187 / 188Two-bid system — technical and financial bids sealed separately, technical opened first; late bids not considered.
Rule 189 / 190Technical bids evaluated by Consultancy Evaluation Committee (CEC), reasons recorded; financial bids opened only for technically qualified bidders.
Rule 192QCBS — quality is prime concern; minimum qualifying technical score in RFP; predefined weightages e.g. 70:30, 60:40, 50:50; highest weighted combined score selected; technical weight never exceeds 80%.
Rule 193LCS (Least Cost System) — standard/routine assignments (audits, engineering design of non-complex works); no technical weightage; lowest evaluated cost selected.
Rule 194Single Source Selection only: natural continuation of previous work, emergency, proprietary technique/sole expertise, or special justification recorded with prior competent authority approval; ensure price reasonableness and no splitting.
Rule 195 / 205Monitor the contract throughout (task-force approach).
Rule 196Public competition for design of symbols/logos — transparent, wide publicity, jury composition notified.
Rule 197Non-Consulting Service — physical, measurable deliverables with clear performance standards: maintenance, vehicle hiring, facilities management, security, janitor, photocopier, drilling, aerial photography, mapping.
Rule 201Up to ₹50 lakh: limited tender enquiry to more than three contractors. Above ₹50 lakh: advertisement on GeM and GeM-CPP (+ own website).
Rule 204Non-consulting service by nomination only in exceptional situations, in consultation with the Financial Adviser, with detailed justification.
Rule 206Anything not covered by Rules 198–205 → refer to Rules 142–176 (goods procedure), NOT the consulting-services rules.
