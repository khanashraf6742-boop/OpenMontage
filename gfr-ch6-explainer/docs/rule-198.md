# Rule 198 — Procurement of Non-consulting Services

*Part B — Procurement of Services · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

A Ministry or Department may procure certain non-consulting services in the interest of economy and efficiency and it may prescribe detailed instructions and procedures for this purpose without, however, contravening the following basic guidelines.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Economy aur efficiency ke liye non-consulting services procure kar sakte hain; detailed instructions de sakte hain lekin neeche diye basic guidelines ke khilaf nahi.

---

Related: Chapter 6 covers Rules 142–206. Rule 206 says circumstances not covered by Rules 198–205 fall back to Rules 142–176 (procurement of goods), **not** the consulting-services rules.
