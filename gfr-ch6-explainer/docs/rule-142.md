# Rule 142 — Scope of the Chapter

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

This chapter contains the general rules applicable to all Ministries or Departments, regarding procurement of goods required for use in the public service. Detailed instructions relating to procurement of goods may be issued by the procuring departments broadly in conformity with the general rules contained in this Chapter.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Chapter 6 ka pehla rule — scope. Saari Central Government Ministries/Departments par lagu hota hai. Department apni khud ki detailed procedures bana sakti hai, lekin unhe in general rules ke "broadly in conformity" mein hona chahiye — yaani in rules ke khilaf nahi ja sakte.
