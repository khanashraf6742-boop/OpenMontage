# Rule 153 — Reserved Items and other Purchase/Price Preference Policy

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

The Central Government, through administrative instructions, has reserved all items of hand spun and handwoven textiles (khadi goods) for exclusive purchase from Khadi Village Industries Commission (KVIC). Of all items of textiles required by Central Government departments, it shall be mandatory to make procurement of at least 20% from amongst items of handloom origin, for exclusive purchase from KVIC and/or Handloom Clusters such as Co-Operative Societies, Self Help Group (SHG) Federations, Joint Liability Group (JLG), Producer Companies (PC), Corporations etc. including Weavers having Pehchan Cards. Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) have notified procurement policy under section 11 of the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006. The Central Government may, by notification, provide for mandatory procurement of any goods or services from any category of bidders, or provide for preference to bidders on the grounds of promotion of locally manufactured goods or locally provided services.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Reserved items: khadi goods sirf KVIC se. Textiles mein kam se kam 20% handloom origin se (KVIC / Handloom Clusters / SHG Federations / JLG / Producer Companies / Pehchan Card weavers). MSME procurement policy Section 11 MSMED Act 2006 ke tehat. Local manufacturing preference notification se aa sakti hai.

## 153(i)

**Verbatim:** All items of hand spun and handwoven textiles (khadi goods) are reserved for exclusive purchase from KVIC. Of all items of textiles required, it shall be mandatory to procure at least 20% from items of handloom origin — KVIC and/or Handloom Clusters such as Co-Operative Societies, SHG Federations, JLG, Producer Companies, Corporations etc. including Weavers having Pehchan Cards.

**Hinglish:** Khadi = sirf KVIC. Textiles ka 20% handloom origin se.

## 153(ii)

**Verbatim:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) have notified procurement policy under section 11 of the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006.

**Hinglish:** MSME procurement policy alag se notified hai.

## 153(iii)

**Verbatim:** The Central Government may, by notification, provide for mandatory procurement of any goods or services from any category of bidders, or provide for preference to bidders on the grounds of promotion of locally manufactured goods or locally provided services.

**Hinglish:** Notification se kisi category ko mandatory ya locally-manufactured ko preference diya ja sakta hai.
