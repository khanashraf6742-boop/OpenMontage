# Rule 188 — Late Bids

*Part B — Procurement of Services · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Late bids i.e. bids received after the specified date and time of receipt should not be considered.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Specified date/time ke baad aaye bids consider nahi hongi.
