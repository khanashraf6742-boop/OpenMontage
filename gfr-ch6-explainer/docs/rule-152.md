# Rule 152 — Enlistment of Indian Agents

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Ministries/Departments if they so require, may enlist Indian agents, who desire to quote directly on behalf of their foreign principals.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Ministry chahe to un Indian agents ko enlist kar sakti hai jo apne foreign principals ki taraf se seedha quote karna chahte hain. Ye "may" hai — discretionary.

## Amendment footnote

Amended vide DoE OM No. F.26/2/2016-PPD dated 25.07.2017.
