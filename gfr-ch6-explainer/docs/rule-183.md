# Rule 183 — Identification of likely sources

*Part B — Procurement of Services · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Where the estimated cost of the consulting service is up to ₹50 lakhs, preparation of a long list of potential consultants may be done on the basis of formal or informal enquiries from other Ministries or Departments and Organisations involved in similar activities. Where the estimated cost of the consulting service is above ₹50 lakhs, in addition to (i) above, an enquiry for seeking ‘Expression of Interest’ from consultants should be published on GeM as well as on GeM-Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP). An organisation having its own website should also publish all its advertised tender enquiries on the website. Enquiry for seeking Expression of Interest should include in brief, the broad scope of work or service, inputs to be provided by the Ministry or Department, eligibility and the prequalification criteria to be met by the consultant(s) and consultant’s past experience in similar work or service. The consultants may also be asked to send their comments on the objectives and scope of the work or service projected in the enquiry. Adequate time should be allowed for getting responses from interested consultants.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

₹50 lakh tak — formal/informal enquiries se long list. ₹50 lakh se upar — EOI publish karna MANDATORY GeM + GeM-CPP par (aur apni website par). EOI mein broad scope, ministry ke inputs, eligibility, prequalification criteria, past experience.

## 183(i)

**Verbatim:** Where the estimated cost of the consulting service is up to ₹50 lakhs, preparation of a long list of potential consultants may be done on the basis of formal or informal enquiries from other Ministries or Departments and Organisations involved in similar activities.

**Hinglish:** ₹50 lakh tak — long list formal/informal enquiries se.

## 183(ii)

**Verbatim:** Where the estimated cost of the consulting service is above ₹50 lakhs, in addition to (i) above, an enquiry for seeking ‘Expression of Interest’ from consultants should be published on GeM as well as on GeM-CPPP. An organisation having its own website should also publish all its advertised tender enquiries on the website. Enquiry for seeking EOI should include in brief the broad scope of work or service, inputs to be provided by the Ministry or Department, eligibility and prequalification criteria, and consultant’s past experience in similar work or service. The consultants may also be asked to send their comments on the objectives and scope. Adequate time should be allowed for getting responses.

**Hinglish:** ₹50 lakh+ → EOI mandatory on GeM + GeM-CPP + own website; EOI ke 6 contents.
