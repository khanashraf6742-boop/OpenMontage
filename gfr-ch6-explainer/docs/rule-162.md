# Rule 162 — Limited Tender Enquiry

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

This method may be adopted when estimated value of the goods to be procured is up to ₹50 lakhs. Copies of the bidding document should be sent directly by speed post/registered post/courier/email to firms which are borne on the list of registered suppliers for the goods in question as referred under Rule 150 above. The number of supplier firms in Limited Tender Enquiry should be more than three. Efforts should be made to identify a higher number of approved suppliers to obtain more responsive bids on competitive basis. Further, an organisation should publish its limited tender enquiries on GeM as well as on GeM-Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP). An organisation having its own website should also publish all its advertised tender enquiries on the website. The unsolicited bids should not be accepted. However, Ministries/Departments should evolve a system by which interested firms can register and bid in next round of tendering. Purchase through Limited Tender Enquiry may be adopted even where the estimated value of the procurement is more than ₹50 lakhs, in the following circumstances: (a) The competent authority in the Ministry or Department certifies that the demand is urgent and any additional expenditure involved by not procuring through advertised tender enquiry is justified in view of the urgency. The Ministry or Department should also put on record the nature of the urgency and reasons why the procurement could not be anticipated. (b) There are sufficient reasons, to be recorded in writing by the competent authority, indicating that it will not be in public interest to procure the goods through advertised tender enquiry. (c) The sources of supply are definitely known and possibility of fresh source(s) beyond those being tapped is remote. Sufficient time should be allowed for submission of bids in Limited Tender Enquiry cases.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

₹50 lakh tak limited tender enquiry — registered suppliers (Rule 150) ko directly bhejo, teen se zyada firms. LTE enquiries bhi GeM + GeM-CPP par publish karni hain. Unsolicited bids accept nahi.

## 162(i)

**Verbatim:** When estimated value of the goods to be procured is up to ₹50 lakhs. Copies of the bidding document should be sent directly by speed post/registered post/courier/email to firms borne on the list of registered suppliers (Rule 150). The number of supplier firms should be more than three. Efforts should be made to identify a higher number of approved suppliers to obtain more responsive bids on competitive basis. An organisation should publish its limited tender enquiries on GeM as well as on GeM-CPPP, and on its own website.

**Hinglish:** ₹50 lakh tak LTE; >3 firms; GeM + GeM-CPP + own website par publish.

## 162(ii)

**Verbatim:** The unsolicited bids should not be accepted. However, Ministries/Departments should evolve a system by which interested firms can register and bid in next round of tendering.

**Hinglish:** Unsolicited bids nahi — par next round ke liye register karne ka system hona chahiye.

## 162(iii)(a)

**Verbatim:** LTE may be adopted even above ₹50 lakhs where the competent authority certifies that the demand is urgent and any additional expenditure involved by not procuring through advertised tender enquiry is justified in view of the urgency. The Ministry or Department should also put on record the nature of the urgency and reasons why the procurement could not be anticipated.

**Hinglish:** Urgency — competent authority certify kare, aur urgency ki nature + kyon anticipate nahi ho saka, record ho.

## 162(iii)(b)

**Verbatim:** There are sufficient reasons, to be recorded in writing by the competent authority, indicating that it will not be in public interest to procure the goods through advertised tender enquiry.

**Hinglish:** Public interest na hone ke likhit reasons.

## 162(iii)(c)

**Verbatim:** The sources of supply are definitely known and possibility of fresh source(s) beyond those being tapped is remote.

**Hinglish:** Supply sources definitely known hon aur naye sources ki sambhavna kam.

## 162(iv)

**Verbatim:** Sufficient time should be allowed for submission of bids in Limited Tender Enquiry cases.

**Hinglish:** LTE mein bhi bid submission ke liye kaafi time do.

## Exception

LTE above ₹50 lakh only in the three circumstances in Rule 162(iii)(a), (b) and (c), each requiring the competent authority to certify/record reasons.

Unsolicited bids are not to be accepted.

Scientific Ministries: LTE up to ₹1 crore (DoE OM dated 05.06.2025).
