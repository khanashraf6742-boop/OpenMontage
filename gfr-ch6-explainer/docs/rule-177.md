# Rule 177 — Definition of "Consulting Service"

*Part B — Procurement of Services · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

“Consulting Service means any subject matter of procurement (which as distinguished from ‘Non-Consultancy Services’ involves primarily non-physical, project-specific, intellectual and procedural processes where outcomes/deliverables would vary from one consultant to another), other than goods or works, except those incidental or consequential to the service, and includes professional, intellectual, training and advisory services or any other service classified or declared as such by a procuring entity but does not include direct engagement of a retired Government servant.” Note: These Services typically involve providing expert or strategic advice e.g., management consultants, policy consultants, communications consultants, Advisory and project related Consulting Services which include, feasibility studies, project management, engineering services, finance, accounting and taxation services, training and development etc.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Consulting service = non-physical, project-specific, intellectual aur procedural kaam, jahan har consultant ka output alag hoga. Professional, intellectual, training, advisory services ismein hain.

## Note / Explanation

Examples: management consultants, policy consultants, communications consultants, feasibility studies, project management, engineering services, finance, accounting and taxation services, training and development.

## Exception

Does NOT include direct engagement of a retired Government servant.

Goods and works are excluded, except those incidental or consequential to the service.
