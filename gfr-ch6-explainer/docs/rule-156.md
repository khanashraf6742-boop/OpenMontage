# Rule 156 — Purchase of goods by obtaining quotations — DELETED

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Rule 156 stands deleted.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Rule 156 (purana "obtaining quotations" rule) delete ho chuka hai. Ab ₹50,000–₹5,00,000 ka purchase Rule 155 (Purchase Committee) se hota hai.

## Exception

The entire Rule 156 has been deleted.

## Amendment footnote

Deleted vide DoE OM No. F.1/26/2018-PPD dated 02.04.2019.
