# Rule 144 — Fundamental principles of public buying (for all procurements including procurement of works)

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Every authority delegated with the financial powers of procuring goods in public interest shall have the responsibility and accountability to bring efficiency, economy, and transparency in matters relating to public procurement and for fair and equitable treatment of suppliers and promotion of competition in public procurement. The procedure to be followed in making public procurement must conform to the following yardsticks:

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Public buying ke 10 yardsticks. Ye "should" ke roop mein hain — inhe follow karna hai, aur inme se kuch (jaise Annual Procurement Plan) "shall" hain. Procurement of works par bhi yehi lagu hote hain.

## 144(i)(a)

**Verbatim:** The description of the subject matter of procurement to the extent practicable should be objective, functional, generic and measurable and specify technical, qualitative and performance characteristics.

**Hinglish:** Specification generic honi chahiye — brand ke naam par nahi.

## 144(i)(b)

**Verbatim:** The description … should not indicate a requirement for a particular trade mark, trade name or brand.

**Hinglish:** Trade mark / trade name / brand ka zikr mana hai.

## 144(ii)

**Verbatim:** The specifications in terms of quality, type etc., as also quantity of goods to be procured, should be clearly spelt out keeping in view the specific needs of the procuring organisations. The specifications so worked out should meet the basic needs of the organisation without including superfluous and non-essential features, which may result in unwarranted expenditure.

**Hinglish:** Sirf zaroorat ke hisaab se specification — extra features = extra kharcha.

## 144(iii)

**Verbatim:** Where applicable, the technical specifications shall, to the extent practicable, be based on the national technical regulations or recognized national standards or building codes, wherever such standards exist, and in their absence, be based on the relevant international standards. In case of Government of India funded projects abroad, the technical specifications may be framed based on requirements and standards of the host beneficiary Government, where such standards exist.

**Hinglish:** Pehle national standards, warna international standards.

## 144(iv)

**Verbatim:** Care should also be taken to avoid purchasing quantities in excess of requirement to avoid inventory carrying costs.

**Hinglish:** Zaroorat se zyada quantity mat lo — inventory carrying cost badhti hai.

## 144(v)

**Verbatim:** Offers should be invited following a fair, transparent and reasonable procedure.

**Hinglish:** Offers fair, transparent aur reasonable procedure se maango.

## 144(vi)

**Verbatim:** The procuring authority should be satisfied that the selected offer adequately meets the requirement in all respects.

**Hinglish:** Selected offer requirement poori tarah meet karti hai — ye satisfy karna zaroori.

## 144(vii)

**Verbatim:** The procuring authority should satisfy itself that the price of the selected offer is reasonable and consistent with the quality required.

**Hinglish:** Price reasonable aur quality ke hisaab se hona chahiye.

## 144(viii)

**Verbatim:** A complete schedule of procurement cycle from date of issuing the tender to date of issuing the contract should be published when the tender is issued.

**Hinglish:** Tender ke saath poora procurement cycle schedule publish karo.

## 144(ix)

**Verbatim:** All Ministries/Departments shall prepare Annual Procurement Plan before the commencement of the year and the same should also be placed on their website.

**Hinglish:** Annual Procurement Plan saal shuru hone se pehle + website par.

## 144(x)

**Verbatim:** Notwithstanding anything contained in these Rules, Department of Expenditure may, by order in writing, impose restrictions, including prior registration and/ or screening, on procurement from bidders from, or bidders having commercial arrangements with an entity from, a country or countries, or a class of countries, on grounds of defence of India, or matters directly or indirectly related thereto including national security; no procurement shall be made in violation of such restrictions.

**Hinglish:** DoE order se kisi desh ya class of countries se procurement par restrictions — prior registration ya screening bhi — defence of India / national security ke grounds par lagu ho sakti hain. Aise restrictions ke khilaf koi procurement nahi.

## Proviso

Rule 144(iii) Proviso: "Provided that a procuring entity may, for reasons to be recorded in writing, adopt any other technical specification." — kisi doosri technical specification apnani ho to wajah likh kar record karni hogi.

## Note / Explanation

Rule 144(iii) proviso is the only escape route in the yardsticks — and it requires reasons to be recorded in writing.

## Exception

Rule 144(x): DoE may, by order in writing, impose restrictions including prior registration and/or screening on procurement from bidders from (or having commercial arrangements with an entity from) a country or class of countries, on grounds of defence of India or matters directly or indirectly related thereto including national security. No procurement shall be made in violation of such restrictions.

## Amendment footnote

Rule 144(x) inserted vide DoE OM No. F.7/10/2021-PPD dated 23.02.2023 (restrictions on bidders from countries sharing a land border).
