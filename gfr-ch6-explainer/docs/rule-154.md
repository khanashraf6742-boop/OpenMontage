# Rule 154 — Purchase of goods without quotation

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Purchase of goods upto the value of ₹50,000 (Rupees fifty thousand only) on each occasion may be made without inviting quotations or bids on the basis of a certificate to be recorded by the competent authority in the following format. "I … am personally satisfied that these goods purchased are of the requisite quality and specification and have been purchased from a reliable supplier at a reasonable price."

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

₹50,000 tak (har occasion par) bina quotation/bids ke purchase — bas competent authority ka personal satisfaction certificate record karna hai. Ye certificate likhna optional nahi, rule ka hissa hai.

## Note / Explanation

Prescribed certificate: "I … am personally satisfied that these goods purchased are of the requisite quality and specification and have been purchased from a reliable supplier at a reasonable price."

## Exception

Scientific Ministries/Departments/Organisations (DST, DBT, DSIR, DAE, Space, MoES, DRDO, ICAR + affiliated institutions & universities, DHR incl. ICMR, and educational/research institutes conducting post-graduate/doctoral courses or research under any Ministry/Department): limit raised to ₹2,00,000 — vide DoE OM No. F.20/42/2021-PPD dated 05.06.2025. Applicable only for scientific equipment and consumables for research purposes, and only when not available on GeM.

## Amendment footnote

Amended vide DoE OM No. F.1/3/2024-PPD dated 10.07.2024 (₹25,000 → ₹50,000).

Special higher limit for Scientific Ministries inserted vide DoE OM No. F.20/42/2021-PPD dated 20.05.2024 and further raised vide DoE OM No. F.20/42/2021-PPD dated 05.06.2025.
