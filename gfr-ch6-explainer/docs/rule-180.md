# Rule 180 — Identification of Services required to be performed by Consultants

*Part B — Procurement of Services · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Engagement of consultants may be resorted to in situations requiring high quality services for which the concerned Ministry/Department does not have requisite expertise. Approval of the competent authority should be obtained before engaging consultant(s).

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Consultant tabhi jab ministry ke paas expertise na ho. Aur competent authority ka approval PEHLE lena zaroori hai.

## 180 — when

**Verbatim:** Situations requiring high quality services for which the concerned Ministry/Department does not have requisite expertise.

**Hinglish:** Expertise na hone par.

## 180 — approval

**Verbatim:** Approval of the competent authority should be obtained before engaging consultant(s).

**Hinglish:** Approval engage karne se PEHLE.
