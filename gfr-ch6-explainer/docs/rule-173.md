# Rule 173 — Transparency, competition, fairness and elimination of arbitrariness in the procurement process

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

All government purchases should be made in a transparent, competitive and fair manner, to secure best value for money. This will also enable the prospective bidders to formulate and send their competitive bids with confidence. Some of the measures for ensuring the above are as follows:

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Sabse lamba rule — 22 sub-points. Yahan se hi Code of Integrity (Rule 175) nikla hai. NIL charges = unresponsive bid, negotiation discouraged, single bid bhi valid ho sakta hai — ye sab yahin hai.

## 173(i)(a)–(i)

**Verbatim:** The text of the bidding document should be self-contained and comprehensive without any ambiguities … The condition of prior turnover and prior experience may be relaxed for Startups … The bidding document should contain: (a) Description and Specifications of goods including nature, quantity, time and place(s) of delivery; (b) criteria for eligibility and qualifications …; (c) eligibility criteria for goods indicating legal restrictions about the origin of goods; (d) procedure, date, time and place for sending the bids; (e) date, time and place of opening of the bid; (f) criteria for evaluation of bids; (g) special terms affecting performance; (h) essential terms of the procurement contract; (i) a clause that "if a firm quotes NIL charges/consideration, the bid shall be treated as unresponsive and will not be considered".

**Hinglish:** Bidding document self-contained; Startups ke liye turnover/experience relax; 9 contents; NIL charge = unresponsive.

## 173(ii)

**Verbatim:** Any other information which the procuring entity considers necessary for the bidders to submit their bids.

**Hinglish:** Jo bhi doosri information bidders ke bid dene ke liye zaroori ho, woh bhi bidding document mein honi chahiye.

## 173(iii)

**Verbatim:** Modification to bidding document: (a) any modification/clarification materially affecting terms shall be published or communicated in the same manner as the initial bidding document; (b) if more time is required, the time limit shall be extended before the last date for submission of bids; (c) any bidder who has submitted a bid shall have the opportunity to modify or resubmit it, or withdraw it in case the modification materially affects the essential terms, within the period initially allotted or extended; provided the bid last submitted or as modified shall be considered for evaluation.

**Hinglish:** Modification same manner mein communicate; time extend; bidder ko modify/resubmit/withdraw ka mauka.

## 173(iv)

**Verbatim:** Suitable provision should be kept in the bidding document to enable a bidder to question the bidding conditions, bidding process and/or rejection of its bid. The reasons for rejecting a tender or non-issuing a tender document to a prospective bidder must be disclosed where enquiries are made by the bidder.

**Hinglish:** Rejection ke reasons disclose karne honge.

## 173(v)–(vii)

**Verbatim:** Suitable provision for settlement of disputes; the bidding document should indicate clearly that the resultant contract will be interpreted under Indian Laws; bidders should be given reasonable time to prepare and send their bids.

**Hinglish:** Dispute settlement; Indian Laws; reasonable time.

## 173(viii)

**Verbatim:** The bids should be opened in public and authorised representatives of the bidders should be permitted to attend the bid opening.

**Hinglish:** Bid opening public — bidders ke authorised representatives attend kar sakte hain.

## 173(ix)

**Verbatim:** The specifications of the required goods should be clearly stated without any ambiguity so that the prospective bidders can send meaningful bids. In order to attract sufficient number of bidders, the specification should be broad based to the extent feasible.

**Hinglish:** Specifications bina ambiguity ke clearly likho taaki bidders meaningful bids de saken; zyada bidders attract karne ke liye specification broad-based rakho.

## 173(x)

**Verbatim:** Pre-bid conference: in case of turn-key contract(s) or contract(s) of special nature for procurement of sophisticated and costly equipment or wherever felt necessary, a suitable provision is to be kept for one or more rounds of pre-bid conference … The records of such conference shall be intimated to all bidders and shall also be exhibited on the website(s) where tender was published.

**Hinglish:** Sophisticated/costly equipment mein pre-bid conference; records sabhi bidders tak + website par.

## 173(xi)

**Verbatim:** Criteria for determining responsiveness: (a) time of delivery; (b) performance/efficiency/environmental characteristics; (c) terms of payment and of guarantees; (d) price; (e) cost of operating, maintaining and repairing etc.

**Hinglish:** Responsiveness ke 5 criteria.

## 173(xii)

**Verbatim:** Bids received should be evaluated in terms of the conditions already incorporated in the bidding documents; no new condition should be brought in for evaluation. Determination of a bid’s responsiveness should be based on the contents of the bid itself without recourse to extrinsic evidence.

**Hinglish:** Naya condition evaluation mein nahi; bid ke contents par hi decide.

## 173(xiii)

**Verbatim:** Bidders should not be permitted to alter or modify their bids after expiry of the deadline for receipt of bids.

**Hinglish:** Deadline ke baad koi modification nahi.

## 173(xiv)

**Verbatim:** Negotiation with bidders after bid opening must be severely discouraged. However, in exceptional circumstances where price negotiation against an ad-hoc procurement is necessary due to some unavoidable circumstances, the same may be resorted to only with the lowest evaluated responsive bidder.

**Hinglish:** Negotiation discouraged; sirf L-1 ke saath exceptional circumstances mein.

## 173(xvi)

**Verbatim:** Contract should ordinarily be awarded to the lowest evaluated bidder whose bid has been found to be responsive and who is eligible and qualified … where the lowest acceptable bidder is not in a position to supply the full quantity, the remaining quantity, as far as possible, be ordered from the next higher responsive bidder at the rates offered by the lowest responsive bidder.

**Hinglish:** Ordinarily L-1 ko award; partial supply par baaki quantity next higher bidder se L-1 rates par.

## 173(xvii)

**Verbatim:** Procurement of Energy Efficient Electrical Appliances: Ministries/Departments while procuring electrical appliances notified by DoE shall ensure that they carry the notified threshold or higher Star Rating of Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE).

**Hinglish:** BEE Star Rating wale appliances.

## 173(xviii)

**Verbatim:** The name of the successful bidder awarded the contract should be mentioned in the CPPP, Ministries or Departments website and their notice board or bulletin.

**Hinglish:** Successful bidder ka naam CPPP + website + notice board par.

## 173(xix)

**Verbatim:** Rejection of all Bids is justified when: (a) effective competition is lacking; (b) all Bids and Proposals are not substantially responsive to the requirements of the Procurement Documents; (c) the Bids’/Proposals’ prices are substantially higher than the updated cost estimate or available budget; or (d) none of the technical Proposals meets the minimum technical qualifying score.

**Hinglish:** Sab bids reject karne ke 4 grounds.

## 173(xx)

**Verbatim:** Lack of competition shall not be determined solely on the basis of the number of Bidders. Even when only one Bid is submitted, the process may be considered valid provided: (a) the procurement was satisfactorily advertised and sufficient time was given; (b) the qualification criteria were not unduly restrictive; and (c) prices are reasonable in comparison to market values.

**Hinglish:** Ek hi bid aaye to bhi process valid ho sakta hai — 3 conditions.

## 173(xxi)

**Verbatim:** When a limited or open tender results in only one effective offer, it shall be treated as a single tender contract.

**Hinglish:** Ek hi effective offer = single tender contract.

## 173(xxii)

**Verbatim:** In case a purchase Committee is constituted to purchase or recommend the procurement, no member of the purchase Committee should be reporting directly to any other member of such Committee in case estimated value of procurement exceeds ₹50 lakhs.

**Hinglish:** ₹50 lakh se upar — committee member doosre member ko directly report na kare.

## Exception

Startups — prior turnover and prior experience conditions may be relaxed, subject to meeting quality & technical specifications.

Sub-rule (xv) has been deleted.

## Amendment footnote

Sub-rule (xv) deleted vide DoE OM No. F.1/26/2018-PPD dated 02.04.2019.

Rule 173(xxii) threshold amended vide DoE OM No. F.1/3/2024-PPD dated 10.07.2024.
