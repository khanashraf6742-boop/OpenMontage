# Rule 161 — Advertised Tender Enquiry

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Subject to exceptions incorporated under Rule 154, 155, 162 and 166, invitation to tenders by advertisement should be used for procurement of goods of estimated value of ₹50 lakhs (Rupees Fifty Lakh) and above. Advertisement in such cases should be given on GeM as well as on GeM-Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP). An organisation having its own website should also publish all its advertised tender enquiries on the website. The organisation should also post the complete bidding document in its website and on CPPP to enable prospective bidders to make use of the document by downloading from the web site. The advertisements for invitation of tenders should give the complete web address from where the bidding documents can be downloaded. In order to promote wider participation and ease of bidding, no cost of tender document may be charged for the tender documents downloaded by the bidders. Ordinarily, the minimum time to be allowed for submission of bids should be three weeks from the date of publication of the tender notice or availability of the bidding document for sale, whichever is later. Where the Department also contemplates obtaining bids from abroad, the minimum period should be kept as four weeks for both domestic and foreign bidders.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

₹50 lakh aur upar ke goods ke liye advertised tender. Advertisement GeM + GeM-CPP par, apni website par bhi. Tender document free download. Minimum 3 hafte (foreign bidders bhi chahiye to 4 hafte).

## 161(i)

**Verbatim:** Invitation to tenders by advertisement should be used for procurement of goods of estimated value of ₹50 lakhs and above. Advertisement should be given on GeM as well as on GeM-Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP). An organisation having its own website should also publish all its advertised tender enquiries on the website.

**Hinglish:** ₹50 lakh+ → advertised tender; GeM + GeM-CPP + own website.

## 161(ii)

**Verbatim:** The organisation should also post the complete bidding document in its website and on CPPP to enable prospective bidders to make use of the document by downloading from the web site.

**Hinglish:** Poora bidding document website aur CPPP par.

## 161(iii)

**Verbatim:** The advertisements for invitation of tenders should give the complete web address from where the bidding documents can be downloaded.

**Hinglish:** Advertisement mein download ka pura web address ho.

## 161(iv)(a)

**Verbatim:** Global Tender Enquiry (GTE): where the Ministry or Department feels that the goods of the required quality, specifications etc., may not be available in the country and it is necessary to also look for suitable competitive offers from abroad, the Ministry or Department may send copies of the tender notice to the Indian Embassies abroad as well as to the Foreign Embassies in India. The selection of embassies will depend on the possibility of availability of the required goods in such countries. In such cases e-procurement as per Rule 160 may not be insisted.

**Hinglish:** GTE — goods desh mein na milne par; Indian Embassies abroad aur Foreign Embassies in India ko tender notice; e-procurement insist nahi ki jaati.

## 161(iv)(b)

**Verbatim:** No Global Tender Enquiry (GTE), however shall be invited for tenders up to ₹200 crore or such limit as may be prescribed by the Department of Expenditure from time to time. Provided that for tenders below such limit, in exceptional cases, where the Ministry or Department feels that there are special reasons for GTE, it may record its detailed justification and seek prior approval for relaxation to the above rule from the Competent Authority specified by the Department of Expenditure.

**Hinglish:** ₹200 crore tak GTE nahi — proviso ke tehat exceptional case mein detailed justification + prior approval.

## 161(v)

**Verbatim:** In order to promote wider participation and ease of bidding, no cost of tender document may be charged for the tender documents downloaded by the bidders.

**Hinglish:** Downloaded tender document par koi charge nahi.

## 161(vi)

**Verbatim:** Ordinarily, the minimum time to be allowed for submission of bids should be three weeks from the date of publication of the tender notice or availability of the bidding document for sale, whichever is later. Where the Department also contemplates obtaining bids from abroad, the minimum period should be kept as four weeks for both domestic and foreign bidders.

**Hinglish:** 3 hafte minimum; foreign bids bhi lene hain to 4 hafte.

## Proviso

Rule 161(iv)(b) Proviso: in exceptional cases below the ₹200 crore limit, detailed justification may be recorded and prior approval for relaxation sought from the Competent Authority specified by DoE.

## Exception

Exceptions under Rules 154, 155, 162 and 166.

GTE up to ₹200 crore prohibited; proviso allows prior approval with detailed justification.

Scientific Ministries: advertised tender for estimated value above ₹1 crore (DoE OM dated 05.06.2025).

## Amendment footnote

Amended vide DoE OM No. F.1/3/2024-PPD dated 10.07.2024 (₹25 lakh → ₹50 lakh).

Rule 161(iv) amended vide DoE OM No. F.12/17/2019-PPD dated 15.05.2020 (GTE ₹200 crore limit).
