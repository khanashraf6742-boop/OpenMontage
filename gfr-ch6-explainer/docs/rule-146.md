# Rule 146 — Procurement of goods required on mobilisation

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Procurement of goods required on mobilisation and/or during the continuance of Military operations shall be regulated by special rules and orders issued by the Government on this behalf from time to time.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Military operations ke dauraan / mobilisation ke liye goods — in par alag special rules aur Government orders lagu hote hain, Chapter 6 ke general rules nahi.

## Exception

Mobilisation and military operations — governed by special rules and orders issued by Government from time to time.
