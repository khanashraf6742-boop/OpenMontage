# Rule 149 — Government e-Market place (GeM)

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Government of India has established the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) for common use Goods and Services. GeM SPV will ensure adequate publicity including periodic advertisement of the items to be procured through GeM for the prospective suppliers. The Procurement of Goods and Services by Ministries or Departments will be mandatory for Goods or Services available on GeM. The credentials of suppliers on GeM shall be certified by GeM SPV. The procuring authorities will certify the reasonability of rates. The GeM portal shall be utilized by the Government buyers for direct online purchases as under:

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

GeM ka pura ladder — value ke hisaab se teen tiers. Ye woh rule hai jo 10.07.2024 ke OM se sabse zyada badla, isliye memory se nahi, latest source se padhna zaroori.

## 149(i)

**Verbatim:** Up to ₹50,000/- through any of the available suppliers on the GeM, meeting the requisite quality, specification and delivery period.

**Hinglish:** ₹50,000 tak — GeM par kisi bhi available supplier se seedha purchase.

## 149(ii)

**Verbatim:** Above ₹50,000/- and up to ₹10,00,000/- through the GeM Seller having lowest price amongst the available sellers, of at least three different manufacturers, on GeM, meeting the requisite quality, specification and delivery period. The tools for online bidding and online reverse auction available on GeM can be used by the Buyer even for procurements less than ₹10,00,000.

**Hinglish:** ₹50,000 se ₹10,00,000 tak — kam se kam 3 different manufacturers mein sabse kam price (L-1) wala GeM seller. ₹10 lakh se neeche bhi online bidding/reverse auction tool use kar sakte hain.

## 149(iii)

**Verbatim:** Above ₹10,00,000/- through the supplier having lowest price meeting the requisite quality, specification and delivery period after mandatorily obtaining bids, using online bidding or reverse auction tool provided on GeM.

**Hinglish:** ₹10,00,000 se upar — online bidding ya reverse auction tool se bids lena MANDATORY hai, phir L-1 supplier select.

## 149(iv)

**Verbatim:** The invitation for the online e-bidding/reverse auction will be available to all the existing Sellers or other Sellers registered on the portal and who have offered their goods/services under the particular product/service category, as per terms and conditions of GeM.

**Hinglish:** Auction ka invitation sabhi registered sellers tak pahunchna chahiye — sirf chune hue sellers tak nahi.

## 149(v)

**Verbatim:** The above-mentioned monetary ceiling is applicable only for purchases made through GeM. For purchases, if any, outside GeM, relevant GFR Rules shall apply.

**Hinglish:** Ye monetary ceilings sirf GeM purchases par lagu hain. GeM ke bahar ki purchase par baaki GFR rules lagu honge.

## 149(vi)

**Verbatim:** The Ministries/Departments shall work out their procurement requirements of Goods and Services on either "OPEX" model or "CAPEX" model as per their requirement/suitability at the time of preparation of Budget Estimates (BE) and shall project their Annual Procurement Plan of goods and services on GeM portal within 30 days of Budget approval.

**Hinglish:** OPEX ya CAPEX model chuno, aur Budget approval ke 30 din ke andar Annual Procurement Plan GeM par project karo.

## 149(vii)

**Verbatim:** The Government Buyers may ascertain the reasonableness of prices before placement of order using the Business Analytics (BA) tools available on GeM including the Last Purchase Price on GeM, Department’s own Last Purchase Price etc.

**Hinglish:** Order se pehle GeM ke Business Analytics tools se price reasonableness check kar sakte hain.

## 149(viii)

**Verbatim:** A demand for goods shall not be divided into small quantities to make piecemeal purchases to avoid procurement through L-1 Buying / bidding / reverse auction on GeM or the necessity of obtaining the sanction of higher authorities required with reference to the estimated value of the total demand.

**Hinglish:** Demand ko tod kar piecemeal purchase karna mana hai — na bidding se bachna, na higher sanction se.

## Note / Explanation

Note to Rule 149(i): "In case of automobiles, procurement under this sub-rule is permitted without any ceiling limit."

## Exception

Automobiles — no ceiling limit under Rule 149(i) as per the Note.

Rule 149(v): ceilings apply only to purchases made through GeM; outside GeM, relevant GFR Rules apply.

## Amendment footnote

Sub-rules (i), (ii), (iii) amended vide DoE OM No. F.1/3/2024-PPD dated 10.07.2024 (₹25,000/₹5,00,000/₹30,00,000 → ₹50,000/₹10,00,000/₹10,00,000).

SOURCE CONFLICT FLAGGED: the official DoE compilation places the "automobiles — no ceiling" Note immediately after sub-rule (i). Some secondary compilations instead show a ₹30 lakh continuation for automobiles inside sub-rules (ii)/(iii). The official compilation position is reproduced here; verify with your department for automobile tenders.
