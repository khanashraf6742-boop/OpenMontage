# Rule 164 — Two-Stage Bidding

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Ministry/Department may procure the subject matter of procurement by the method of two-stage bidding, if (a) it is not feasible to formulate detailed specifications or identify specific characteristics for the subject matter of procurement, without receiving inputs regarding its technical aspects from bidders; or (b) the character of the subject matter of procurement is subject to rapid technological advances or market fluctuations or both; or (c) Ministry/Department seeks to enter into a contract for the purpose of research, experiment, study or development, except where the contract includes the production of items in quantities sufficient to establish their commercial viability or to recover research and development costs; or (d) The bidder is expected to carry out a detailed survey or investigation and undertake a comprehensive assessment of risks, costs and obligations associated with the particular procurement. The procedure for two stage bidding shall include the following: (a) in the first stage … the Ministry/Department shall invite bids through advertised tender containing the technical aspects and contractual terms and conditions of the proposed procurement without a bid price; (b) all first stage bids, which are otherwise eligible, shall be evaluated through an appropriate committee constituted by the Ministry/Department; (c) the committee may hold discussions with the bidders and if any such discussions is held, equal opportunity shall be given to all bidders to participate in the discussions; (d) in revising the relevant terms and conditions of the procurement, the procuring entity shall not modify the fundamental nature of the procurement itself, but may add, amend or omit any specification of the subject matter of procurement or criterion for evaluation; (e) in the second stage … the procuring entity shall invite bids from all those bidders whose bids at the first stage were not rejected, to present final bid with bid prices in response to a revised set of terms and conditions; (f) any bidder, invited to bid but not in a position to supply the subject matter of procurement due to modification in the specifications or terms and conditions, may withdraw from the bidding proceedings without forfeiting any bid security … by declaring his intention to withdraw from the procurement proceedings with adequate justification.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Two-stage bidding tab jab specifications bidders ke inputs ke bina formulate na ho saken, technology tezi se badal rahi ho, R&D contract ho, ya detailed survey/risk assessment chahiye ho.

## 164(i)(a)

**Verbatim:** Not feasible to formulate detailed specifications or identify specific characteristics without receiving inputs regarding technical aspects from bidders.

**Hinglish:** Specification bina bidder input ke nahi ban sakti.

## 164(i)(b)

**Verbatim:** The character of the subject matter of procurement is subject to rapid technological advances or market fluctuations or both.

**Hinglish:** Technology/market tezi se badalti ho.

## 164(i)(c)

**Verbatim:** Ministry/Department seeks to enter into a contract for the purpose of research, experiment, study or development, except where the contract includes production of items in quantities sufficient to establish commercial viability or to recover R&D costs.

**Hinglish:** R&D/experiment/study contract.

## 164(i)(d)

**Verbatim:** The bidder is expected to carry out a detailed survey or investigation and undertake a comprehensive assessment of risks, costs and obligations associated with the particular procurement.

**Hinglish:** Detailed survey/risk assessment chahiye.

## 164(ii)(a)

**Verbatim:** First stage: invite bids through advertised tender containing technical aspects and contractual terms and conditions of the proposed procurement without a bid price.

**Hinglish:** Stage 1 — bina price ke advertised tender.

## 164(ii)(b)

**Verbatim:** All first stage bids, which are otherwise eligible, shall be evaluated through an appropriate committee constituted by the Ministry/Department.

**Hinglish:** Stage 1 bids appropriate committee evaluate karegi.

## 164(ii)(c)

**Verbatim:** The committee may hold discussions with the bidders and if any such discussions is held, equal opportunity shall be given to all bidders to participate in the discussions.

**Hinglish:** Discussion ho to sabhi bidders ko barabar mauka.

## 164(ii)(d)

**Verbatim:** In revising the relevant terms and conditions, the procuring entity shall not modify the fundamental nature of the procurement itself, but may add, amend or omit any specification or criterion for evaluation.

**Hinglish:** Fundamental nature nahi badal sakta; specification/criterion badal sakti hai.

## 164(ii)(e)

**Verbatim:** Second stage: invite bids from all those bidders whose first-stage bids were not rejected, to present final bid with bid prices in response to a revised set of terms and conditions.

**Hinglish:** Stage 2 — non-rejected bidders ko revised terms ke saath final price bid.

## 164(ii)(f)

**Verbatim:** Any bidder invited to bid but not in a position to supply due to modification in specifications or terms and conditions may withdraw without forfeiting any bid security, by declaring his intention to withdraw with adequate justification.

**Hinglish:** Specification badalne par bina penalty ke withdraw kar sakte hain.
