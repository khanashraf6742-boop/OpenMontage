# Rule 200 — Preparation of Tender enquiry

*Part B — Procurement of Services · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Ministry or Department should prepare a tender enquiry containing, inter alia: (i) The details of the work or service to be performed by the contractor; (ii) The facilities and the inputs which will be provided to the contractor by the Ministry or Department; (iii) Eligibility and qualification criteria to be met by the contractor for performing the required work/service; and (iv) The statutory and contractual obligations to be complied with by the contractor.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Tender enquiry ke 4 contents — work/service details, ministry ke facilities/inputs, eligibility/qualification criteria, statutory & contractual obligations.

## 200(i)

**Verbatim:** The details of the work or service to be performed by the contractor.

**Hinglish:** Kaam ki details.

## 200(ii)

**Verbatim:** The facilities and the inputs which will be provided to the contractor by the Ministry or Department.

**Hinglish:** Ministry ke facilities/inputs.

## 200(iii)

**Verbatim:** Eligibility and qualification criteria to be met by the contractor for performing the required work/service.

**Hinglish:** Eligibility/qualification criteria.

## 200(iv)

**Verbatim:** The statutory and contractual obligations to be complied with by the contractor.

**Hinglish:** Statutory aur contractual obligations.
