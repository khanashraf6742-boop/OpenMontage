# Rule 166 — Single Tender Enquiry

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Procurement from a single source may be resorted to in the following circumstances: (i) It is in the knowledge of the user department that only a particular firm is the manufacturer of the required goods. (ii) In a case of emergency, the required goods are necessarily to be purchased from a particular source and the reason for such decision is to be recorded and approval of competent authority obtained. (iii) For standardization of machinery or spare parts to be compatible to the existing sets of equipment (on the advice of a competent technical expert and approved by the competent authority), the required item is to be purchased only from a selected firm.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Single source sirf teen haalaton mein. (i) aur (iii) mein Proprietary Article Certificate (PAC) chahiye. Emergency mein reason record + competent authority approval.

## 166(i)

**Verbatim:** It is in the knowledge of the user department that only a particular firm is the manufacturer of the required goods.

**Hinglish:** Sirf ek hi firm manufacturer hai — PAC required.

## 166(ii)

**Verbatim:** In a case of emergency, the required goods are necessarily to be purchased from a particular source and the reason for such decision is to be recorded and approval of competent authority obtained.

**Hinglish:** Emergency — reason record + competent authority approval.

## 166(iii)

**Verbatim:** For standardization of machinery or spare parts to be compatible to the existing sets of equipment (on the advice of a competent technical expert and approved by the competent authority), the required item is to be purchased only from a selected firm.

**Hinglish:** Standardization — competent technical expert ki advice + competent authority approval — PAC required.

## Note / Explanation

Proprietary Article Certificate (PAC) in the following form is to be provided by the Ministry/Department before procuring goods from a single source under sub-Rule 166(i) and 166(iii): (i) The indented goods are manufactured by M/s …; (ii) No other make or model is acceptable for the following reasons: …; (iii) Concurrence of finance wing to the proposal vide: …; (iv) Approval of the competent authority vide: … (Signature with date and designation of the indenting officer).

## Exception

PAC is required for sub-Rules 166(i) and 166(iii) only — not for 166(ii) (emergency).
