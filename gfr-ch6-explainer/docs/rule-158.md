# Rule 158 — Purchase of goods by obtaining bids

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Except in cases covered under Rule 154 and 155, Ministries or Departments shall procure goods under the powers referred to in Rule 140 above by following the standard method of obtaining bids in: (i) Advertised Tender Enquiry (ii) Limited Tender Enquiry (iii) Two-Stage Bidding (iv) Single Tender Enquiry (v) Electronic Reverse Auctions.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Rule 154/155 ke cases ko chhod kar, baaki sab procurement in paanch standard methods se hogi.

## 158(i)

**Verbatim:** Advertised Tender Enquiry

**Hinglish:** Rule 161

## 158(ii)

**Verbatim:** Limited Tender Enquiry

**Hinglish:** Rule 162

## 158(iii)

**Verbatim:** Two-Stage Bidding

**Hinglish:** Rule 164

## 158(iv)

**Verbatim:** Single Tender Enquiry

**Hinglish:** Rule 166

## 158(v)

**Verbatim:** Electronic Reverse Auctions

**Hinglish:** Rule 167

## Exception

Procurements covered by Rule 154 (without quotation) and Rule 155 (purchase committee) are outside Rule 158.
