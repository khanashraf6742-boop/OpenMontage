# Rule 206 — Circumstances not covered

*Part B — Procurement of Services · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Any circumstances which are not covered in Rule 198 to Rule 205 for procurement of non-consulting services, the procuring entity may refer Rule 142 to Rule 176 pertaining to procurement of goods and not to the procurement of consulting services.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Rules 198–205 mein na aane wali koi bhi situation → Rules 142–176 (GOODS) apply honge, consulting services ke rules nahi.

## Exception

Cross-reference: non-consulting services not covered by Rules 198–205 fall back to the GOODS rules (Rules 142–176), not the consulting-services rules.
