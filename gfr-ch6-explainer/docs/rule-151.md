# Rule 151 — Debarment from bidding

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

A bidder shall be debarred if he has been convicted of an offence—(a) under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988; or (b) the Indian Penal Code or any other law for the time being in force, for causing any loss of life or property or causing a threat to public health as part of execution of a public procurement contract. A bidder debarred under sub-section (i) or any successor of the bidder shall not be eligible to participate in a procurement process of any procuring entity for a period not exceeding three years commencing from the date of debarment. Department of Expenditure will maintain such list which will also be displayed on the Central Public Procurement Portal. A procuring entity may debar a bidder or any of its successors, from participating in any procurement process undertaken by it, for a period not exceeding two years, if it determines that the bidder has breached the code of integrity. The Ministry/Department will maintain such list which will also be displayed on their website. The bidder shall not be debarred unless such bidder has been given a reasonable opportunity to represent against such debarment.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Debarment ke do alag raste: (1) conviction par — kisi bhi procuring entity se 3 saal tak (DoE list, CPPP par); (2) code of integrity todne par — us entity se 2 saal tak (Ministry list, website par). Dono mein "reasonable opportunity to represent" dena zaroori hai.

## 151(i)(a)

**Verbatim:** A bidder shall be debarred if he has been convicted of an offence under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.

**Hinglish:** PC Act 1988 ki conviction — automatic debarment ground.

## 151(i)(b)

**Verbatim:** … or the Indian Penal Code or any other law for the time being in force, for causing any loss of life or property or causing a threat to public health as part of execution of a public procurement contract.

**Hinglish:** IPC/any law — jaan-maal ki haani ya public health ko khatra, procurement contract ke execution ke dauran.

## 151(ii)

**Verbatim:** A bidder debarred under sub-section (i) or any successor of the bidder shall not be eligible to participate in a procurement process of any procuring entity for a period not exceeding three years commencing from the date of debarment. Department of Expenditure will maintain such list which will also be displayed on the Central Public Procurement Portal.

**Hinglish:** 3 saal tak — kisi bhi procuring entity se. List DoE rakhta hai, CPPP par display.

## 151(iii)

**Verbatim:** A procuring entity may debar a bidder or any of its successors, from participating in any procurement process undertaken by it, for a period not exceeding two years, if it determines that the bidder has breached the code of integrity. The Ministry/Department will maintain such list which will also be displayed on their website.

**Hinglish:** Code of integrity ka breach — 2 saal tak us entity se. List Ministry rakhti hai, website par.

## 151(iv)

**Verbatim:** The bidder shall not be debarred unless such bidder has been given a reasonable opportunity to represent against such debarment.

**Hinglish:** Bina reasonable opportunity ke debarment nahi ho sakta.
