# Rule 194 — Single Source Selection / Consultancy by nomination

*Part B — Procurement of Services · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

The selection by direct negotiation/nomination, on the lines of Single Tender mode of procurement of goods, is considered appropriate only under exceptional circumstance such as: (i) tasks that represent a natural continuation of previous work carried out by the firm; (ii) in case of an emergency situation, situations arising after natural disasters, situations where timely completion of the assignment is of utmost importance; and (iii) situations where execution of the assignment may involve use of proprietary techniques or only one consultant has requisite expertise. (iv) Under some special circumstances, it may become necessary to select a particular consultant where adequate justification is available for such single-source selection in the context of the overall interest of the Ministry or Department. Full justification for single source selection should be recorded in the file and approval of the competent authority obtained before resorting to such single-source selection. (v) It shall ensure fairness and equity, and shall have a procedure in place to ensure that the prices are reasonable and consistent with market rates for tasks of a similar nature; and the required consultancy services are not split into smaller sized procurement.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Single source sirf 4 exceptional grounds par. (iv) mein poori justification file mein record + competent authority ka prior approval. (v) price reasonableness + no splitting.

## 194(i)

**Verbatim:** Tasks that represent a natural continuation of previous work carried out by the firm.

**Hinglish:** Previous work ka natural continuation.

## 194(ii)

**Verbatim:** Emergency situation, situations arising after natural disasters, situations where timely completion of the assignment is of utmost importance.

**Hinglish:** Emergency / natural disaster / timely completion.

## 194(iii)

**Verbatim:** Situations where execution of the assignment may involve use of proprietary techniques or only one consultant has requisite expertise.

**Hinglish:** Proprietary technique ya sole expertise.

## 194(iv)

**Verbatim:** Under some special circumstances … where adequate justification is available in the context of the overall interest of the Ministry or Department. Full justification for single source selection should be recorded in the file and approval of the competent authority obtained before resorting to such single-source selection.

**Hinglish:** Justification file mein + prior approval.

## 194(v)

**Verbatim:** It shall ensure fairness and equity, and shall have a procedure in place to ensure that the prices are reasonable and consistent with market rates for tasks of a similar nature; and the required consultancy services are not split into smaller sized procurement.

**Hinglish:** Price reasonableness + services ko split nahi karna.

## Exception

Single source selection only under exceptional circumstances listed in (i)–(iv), with full justification recorded in the file and prior approval of the competent authority.
