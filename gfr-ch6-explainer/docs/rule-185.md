# Rule 185 — Preparation of Terms of Reference (TOR)

*Part B — Procurement of Services · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

The TOR should include (i) Precise statement of objectives. (ii) Outline of the tasks to be carried out. (iii) Schedule for completion of tasks. (iv) The support or inputs to be provided by the Ministry or Department to facilitate the consultancy. (v) The final outputs that will be required of the Consultant.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

TOR ke paanch ang — objectives, tasks, schedule, ministry ke inputs, final outputs.

## 185(i)

**Verbatim:** Precise statement of objectives.

**Hinglish:** Objectives.

## 185(ii)

**Verbatim:** Outline of the tasks to be carried out.

**Hinglish:** Tasks ka outline.

## 185(iii)

**Verbatim:** Schedule for completion of tasks.

**Hinglish:** Schedule.

## 185(iv)

**Verbatim:** The support or inputs to be provided by the Ministry or Department to facilitate the consultancy.

**Hinglish:** Ministry ke inputs.

## 185(v)

**Verbatim:** The final outputs that will be required of the Consultant.

**Hinglish:** Final outputs.
