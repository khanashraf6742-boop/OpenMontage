# Rule 172(1) — Advance payment to supplier

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Ordinarily, payments for services rendered or supplies made should be released only after the services have been rendered or supplies made. However, it may become necessary to make advance payments for example in the following types of cases: (i) Advance payment demanded by firms holding maintenance contracts for servicing of Air-conditioners, computers, other costly equipment, etc. (ii) Advance payment demanded by firms against fabrication contracts, turn-key contracts etc. Such advance payments should not exceed the following limits: (a) Thirty per cent of the contract value to private firms; (b) Forty per cent of the contract value to a State or Central Government agency or a Public Sector Undertaking; or (c) in case of maintenance contract, the amount should not exceed the amount payable for six months under the contract. Ministries or Departments of the Central Government may relax, in consultation with their Financial Advisers concerned, the ceilings (including percentage laid down for advance payment for private firms) mentioned above. While making any advance payment as above, adequate safeguards in the form of bank guarantee etc. should be obtained from the firm.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Ordinarily payment ke baad hi. Advance do situations mein: maintenance contracts (AC, computers, costly equipment) aur fabrication/turn-key contracts. Ceilings: 30% private firms, 40% Govt agency/PSU, maintenance contract ≤ 6 months value. Bank guarantee safeguards.

## 172(1)(i)

**Verbatim:** Advance payment demanded by firms holding maintenance contracts for servicing of Air-conditioners, computers, other costly equipment, etc.

**Hinglish:** Maintenance contract firms.

## 172(1)(ii)

**Verbatim:** Advance payment demanded by firms against fabrication contracts, turn-key contracts etc.

**Hinglish:** Fabrication / turn-key contracts.

## 172(1)(a)

**Verbatim:** Thirty per cent of the contract value to private firms.

**Hinglish:** Private firms — max 30%.

## 172(1)(b)

**Verbatim:** Forty per cent of the contract value to a State or Central Government agency or a Public Sector Undertaking.

**Hinglish:** Govt agency / PSU — max 40%.

## 172(1)(c)

**Verbatim:** In case of maintenance contract, the amount should not exceed the amount payable for six months under the contract.

**Hinglish:** Maintenance contract — 6 mahine ke amount tak.

## 172(1) — relaxation

**Verbatim:** Ministries or Departments may relax, in consultation with their Financial Advisers concerned, the ceilings (including percentage for private firms). While making any advance payment, adequate safeguards in the form of bank guarantee etc. should be obtained.

**Hinglish:** FA se consult kar ke ceilings relax ho sakti hain; bank guarantee safeguard.
