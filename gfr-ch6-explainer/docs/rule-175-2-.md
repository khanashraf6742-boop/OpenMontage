# Rule 175(2) — Action on breach of the Code of Integrity

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

The procuring entity, after giving a reasonable opportunity of being heard, comes to the conclusion that a bidder or prospective bidder, as the case may be, has contravened the code of integrity, may take appropriate measures.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Code of integrity todne par bhi pehle reasonable opportunity of being heard deni hogi, phir appropriate measures.
