# Rule 168 — Contents of Bidding Document

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

All the terms, conditions, stipulations and information to be incorporated in the bidding document are to be shown in the appropriate chapters as below: Chapter-1: Instructions to Bidders. Chapter-2: Conditions of Contract. Chapter-3: Schedule of Requirements. Chapter-4: Specifications and allied Technical Details. Chapter-5: Price Schedule (to be utilised by the bidders for quoting their prices). Chapter-6: Contract Form. Chapter-7: Other Standard Forms, if any, to be utilised by the purchaser and the bidders.
