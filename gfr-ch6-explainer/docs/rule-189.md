# Rule 189 — Evaluation of Technical Bids

*Part B — Procurement of Services · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

Technical bids should be analysed and evaluated by a Consultancy Evaluation Committee (CEC) constituted by the Ministry or Department. The CEC shall record in detail the reasons for acceptance or rejection of the technical proposals analysed and evaluated by it.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

Technical bids Consultancy Evaluation Committee (CEC) evaluate karti hai, aur acceptance/rejection ke wajahat detail mein record karti hai.

## 189 — CEC

**Verbatim:** Technical bids analysed and evaluated by a Consultancy Evaluation Committee (CEC) constituted by the Ministry or Department.

**Hinglish:** CEC ministry constitute karti hai.

## 189 — recording

**Verbatim:** The CEC shall record in detail the reasons for acceptance or rejection of the technical proposals analysed and evaluated by it.

**Hinglish:** Wajahat detail mein record karni hain.
