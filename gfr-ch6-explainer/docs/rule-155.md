# Rule 155 — Purchase of goods by Purchase Committee

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

In case a certain item is not available on the GeM portal, purchase of goods costing above ₹50,000 (Rupees Fifty thousand only) and upto ₹5,00,000/- (Rupees Five lakh only) on each occasion may be made on the recommendations of a duly constituted Local Purchase Committee consisting of three members of an appropriate level as decided by the Head of the Department. The committee will survey the market to ascertain the reasonableness of rate, quality and specifications and identify the appropriate supplier. Before recommending placement of the purchase order, the members of the committee will jointly record a certificate as under: "Certified that we, members of the purchase committee are jointly and individually satisfied that the goods recommended for purchase are of the requisite specification and quality, priced at the prevailing market rate and the supplier recommended is reliable and competent to supply the goods in question, and it is not debarred by Department of Expenditure or Ministry/Department concerned."

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

₹50,000 se zyada aur ₹5,00,000 tak — sirf jab item GeM par available na ho. Teen members ka Local Purchase Committee banega, market survey karega, aur joint certificate dega. Certificate mein "not debarred" ka confirmation bhi hai.

## 155 — LPC composition

**Verbatim:** A duly constituted Local Purchase Committee consisting of three members of an appropriate level as decided by the Head of the Department.

**Hinglish:** 3 members, level Head of Department decide karega.

## 155 — Market survey

**Verbatim:** The committee will survey the market to ascertain the reasonableness of rate, quality and specifications and identify the appropriate supplier.

**Hinglish:** Market survey karke rate/quality/specification ki reasonableness aur supplier identify karo.

## 155 — Joint certificate

**Verbatim:** Certified that we, members of the purchase committee are jointly and individually satisfied that the goods recommended for purchase are of the requisite specification and quality, priced at the prevailing market rate and the supplier recommended is reliable and competent to supply the goods in question, and it is not debarred by Department of Expenditure or Ministry/Department concerned.

**Hinglish:** Joint + individual satisfaction, prevailing market rate, reliable & competent supplier, aur supplier debarred na ho.

## Exception

Scientific Ministries: ₹2,00,000 to ₹25,00,000 (DoE OM No. F.20/42/2021-PPD dated 05.06.2025).

Applies only where the item is NOT available on the GeM portal (condition inserted vide OM dated 02.04.2019).

## Amendment footnote

"In case a certain item is not available on the GeM portal" inserted vide DoE OM No. F.1.26/2018-PPD dated 02.04.2019.

Special higher limits for Scientific Ministries vide DoE OM No. F.20/42/2021-PPD dated 20.05.2024 and 05.06.2025.
