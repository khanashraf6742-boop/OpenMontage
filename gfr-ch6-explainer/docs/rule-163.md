# Rule 163 — Two bid system (simultaneous receipt of separate technical and financial bids)

*Part A — Procurement of Goods · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

For purchasing high value plant, machinery etc. of a complex and technical nature, bids may be obtained in two parts as under: (i) Technical bid consisting of all technical details along with commercial terms and conditions; and (ii) Financial bid indicating itemwise price for the items mentioned in the technical bid. The technical bid and the financial bid should be sealed by the bidder in separate covers duly super-scribed and both these sealed covers are to be put in a bigger cover which should also be sealed and duly super-scribed. The technical bids are to be opened by the purchasing Ministry or Department at the first instance and evaluated by a competent committee or authority. At the second stage financial bids of only these technically acceptable offers should be opened after intimating them the date and time of opening the financial bid for further evaluation and ranking before awarding the contract.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

High value, complex plant/machinery ke liye two-bid. Technical aur financial alag sealed covers, dono ek bade cover mein. Technical pehle khulta hai; financial sirf technically acceptable offers ka.

## 163(i)

**Verbatim:** Technical bid consisting of all technical details along with commercial terms and conditions.

**Hinglish:** Technical bid = technical details + commercial terms.

## 163(ii)

**Verbatim:** Financial bid indicating itemwise price for the items mentioned in the technical bid.

**Hinglish:** Financial bid = itemwise price.

## 163 — procedure

**Verbatim:** Technical and financial bids sealed in separate duly super-scribed covers; both put in a bigger sealed, super-scribed cover. Technical bids opened first and evaluated by a competent committee or authority. At the second stage, financial bids of only technically acceptable offers are opened after intimating them the date and time, for further evaluation and ranking before awarding the contract.

**Hinglish:** Alag covers → technical pehle → financial sirf technically acceptable offers ka.
