# Rule 193 — Least Cost System (LCS)

*Part B — Procurement of Services · GFR 2017 Chapter 6*

## Verbatim text

LCS is appropriate for assignments of a standard or routine nature (such as audits and engineering design of non-complex works) where well established methodologies, practices and standards exist. Unlike QCBS, there is no weightage for Technical score in the final evaluation and the responsive technically qualified proposal with the lowest evaluated cost shall be selected.

## Samajhne mein aasan (Hinglish)

LCS standard/routine kaam ke liye (audit, non-complex engineering design). Technical score ka koi weight nahi — lowest evaluated cost select.

## Exception

Only for standard or routine assignments where well-established methodologies, practices and standards exist.
