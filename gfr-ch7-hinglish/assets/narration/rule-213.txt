Rule 213 — Physical verification of Fixed Assets and Consumables. Yaad rakhne ka formula: Ek saal, Dono category, Custodian saamne, Certificate, Discrepancy → R.33–38. Rule 213 teen sub-rules ka hai: 213(1) fixed assets, 213(2) consumables, 213(3) verification ka procedure (teen clauses). Isi rule se verification ki poori kahani chalti hai. Rule 213(1): Teen baatein: (a) fixed assets ka inventory ordinarily site par rahega — matlab jahan asset hai wahin record bhi, alag jagah file mein nahi; (b) verification kam se kam saal mein ek baar; (c) verification ka nateeja corresponding register mein darj ho. Discrepancy mile to तुरंत investigate karke account mein laya jaaye. "Ordinarily" aur "at least" dono yaad rakhiye — pehla exception ka chance rakhta hai, doosra minimum frequency hai (zyada baar kar sakte hain). Rule 213(2): Consumables ka bhi wahi frequency — saal mein kam se kam ek baar — lekin nateeja stock register mein darj hoga, aur wahan se aage competent authority action legi. Farq samjhiye: 213(1) mein discrepancy "brought to account" hoti hai; 213(2) mein discrepancy "recorded in stock register for appropriate action by the competent authority". Consumables mein competent authority ka role explicit hai. Rule 213(3): Verification kaise hogi — teen rules: (i) verification hamesha us officer ke saamne jo us inventory ka custodian hai (self-verification nahi, doosra officer verify kare par custodian saamne ho); (ii) verification certificate findings ke saath stock register mein darj ho; (iii) verification mein jo shortage, damage ya unserviceable mile, use तुरंत competent authority ke notice mein laaya jaaye — aur action Rules 33 se 38 ke under hoga. Note. Rule 213(3)(iii) Chapter 7 ko Chapter 2 ke "Defalcation and losses" (Rules 33–38) se jodta hai — shortage ka matlab automatic penalty nahi, balki reporting chain shuru hona hai. "At least once in a year" minimum hai — department chahe to zyada baar (jaise half-yearly) karwa sakta hai. Exception. "Inventory for fixed assets shall ordinarily be maintained at site" — "ordinarily" exception ka chance rakhta hai (jaise centralized asset register with site-wise sub-records). Exam trap: Fixed assets aur consumables — dono ki verification frequency? Dono "at least once in a year" (213(1) aur 213(2)). Exam trap: Verification kiski saamne hogi? Us officer ki jo inventory ka custodian hai — Rule 213(3)(i), "shall always".
