Rule 222 — Sale Account.

Yaad rakhne ka formula: Form GFR-11 · Sale supervise karne wale officer ke sign.

Rule 222 ek line ka rule hai — disposal ke baad ka antim record. Chhota par audit mein sabse pehle yahi maanga jaata hai.

Rule 222: Dispose kiye gaye goods ka sale account Form GFR-11 mein banega, aur us par us officer ke sign honge jisne sale ya auction ki supervision ki. Dhyan dein: sign karne wala wohi officer hai jisne supervise kiya — koi aur officer "on behalf" sign nahi kar sakta is rule ke under.

Note. Rule 222 kisi bhi mode ke baad lagta hai — tender ho, auction ho ya scrap sale.

Exam trap: Sale account kaun sign karta hai? Woh officer jisne sale/auction supervise kiya.

Exam trap: Kaunsa form? Form GFR-11 (Rule 217 ka GFR-10 alag hai — woh report of stores for disposal).
