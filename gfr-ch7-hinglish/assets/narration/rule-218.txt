Rule 218 — Modes of Disposal. Yaad rakhne ka formula: ₹4 lakh = Tender ya Auction, ₹2 lakh = 10.07.2024 se pehle. Rule 218 chaar clauses ka hai aur amended hai: clause (i) ki threshold DoE OM F.1/3/2024-PPD (10.07.2024) se ₹2 lakh se badhkar ₹4 lakh ho gayi. Clause (i): Assessed residual value (na ki original purchase value) ₹4,00,000 se upar ho to disposal sirf do tareeqon se: (a) advertised tender se bids, ya (b) public auction. DONO mein se koi ek chunna hai — teesra raasta (direct sale, quotation, committee decision) allowed nahi. Amendment: 10.07.2024 se pehle yah line ₹2,00,000 thi; DoE OM No. F.1/3/2024-PPD dated 10.07.2024 ne ise ₹4,00,000 kar diya. Clause (ii): ₹4 lakh se kam residual value par mode competent authority tay karegi, lekin bina reason ke nahi — teen objectives dhyan mein rakhne honge: (1) samaan ka jama hona rokna, (2) jagah block na hona, (3) disposal hone wale samaan ki value kharab na hona. Saath hi Ministries/Departments ko jahan tak ho sake aise goods ki list banani chahiye — isi list se Special Campaign ke scrap targets bante hain. Clause (iii): Kuch cheezein bechi nahi ja sakti — expired medicines, food grain, ammunition jaise hazardous ya human consumption ke layak na hone wale items ko तुरंत dispose ya destroy karna hai, suitable mode se. Teen wajah: health hazard, environmental pollution, aur misuse ki sambhavna. Yahan koi threshold lagta nahi — ₹4 lakh se kam ho ya zyada, ye clause alag hai. Clause (iv): Jin cheezon mein security concern ho — currency, negotiable instruments, receipt books, stamps, security press waghaira — unka disposal/destruction aise tareeqe se hoga jo official secrets ke rules aur financial prudence dono ka palan kare. Matlab inhein bhi aam auction mein nahi daala jaata; controlled destruction hi raasta hai. Note. Clause (i) aur (ii) dono mein threshold ek hi hai aur dono amendment ke saath badalte hain — 10.07.2024 se pehle dono jagah ₹2 lakh tha. Threshold assessed residual value par hai — original purchase price ya book value par nahi. Exception. (iii) hazardous/unfit items — in par threshold lagta nahi, immediate destruction hi rule hai. (iv) security-sensitive items — official secrets rules ke under alag handling. Amendment. [object Object] Exam trap: Current threshold kya hai? ₹4,00,000 assessed residual value (10.07.2024 se).
