Rule 211 — Lists and Accounts.

Yaad rakhne ka formula: Actual balance = Book balance, kisi bhi waqt check ho sake.

Rule 211 do sub-rules aur ek Note mein hai. (i) general requirement — item-wise lists/accounts; (ii) chaar alag-alag registers; Note — forms mein aur detail jodi ja sakti hai.

Clause (i): Do duties: item-wise lists + accounts rakhna, aur accurate returns banana. Asal test ye hai: kisi bhi waqt asal balance (physical) aur book balance (register) ki tulna ho sake. Register ka format fixed nahi — woh teen cheezon par depend karega: goods ki nature, transactions ki frequency, aur us Ministry/Department ki special requirements. Isliye ek hospital ka drug store register aur ek CPWD store ka register alag dikhte hain — dono valid hain.

Clause (ii): Chaar category ke liye alag-alag accounts — ek hi register sab kuch nahi. (a) Fixed assets — plant, machinery, equipment, furniture, fixtures — Form GFR-22 (Register of Fixed Assets); (b) Consumables — office stationery, chemicals, maintenance spare parts — Form GFR-23 (Stock Register of consumables); (c) Library books — Form GFR 18 (Accession Register); (d) Historical/artistic value wali assets (museum/government departments ke paas) — Form GFR-24. Har category ka behaviour alag hai — consumables ghat-te hain, fixed assets depreciate hote hain, library books ki loss norm alag hai — isliye register alag. Verified: GFR-18 = Accession Register (official DoE GFR forms list, page 179) — yahi library books ka register hai.

Exam trap: Kaunsa form kis asset ke liye? GFR-22 Register of Fixed Assets · GFR-23 Stock Register of consumables · GFR-18 Accession Register (library books) · GFR-24 Register of assets of historical/artistic value.
