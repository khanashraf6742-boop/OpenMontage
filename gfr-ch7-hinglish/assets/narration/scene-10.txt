Tender bhi laga, auction bhi — koi kharidar nahi mila. Ab?

Rule 221 — scrap value par bech sakte hain, competent authority ki approval se aur Finance division se consultation ke baad. Phir bhi na bike to eco-friendly destruction.

Aur write-off alag hai — Rule 223. Sanction tab bhi chahiye jab accounts mein koi adjustment ho ya na ho.

Rule 221 — Agar advertised tender aur auction dono ke bawajood item nahi bikta, to competent authority ki approval se aur Finance division ke saath consultation karke scrap value par becha ja sakta hai. Agar scrap value par bhi na bike, to kisi aur mode se dispose kiya ja sakta hai — jismein eco-friendly destruction bhi shaamil hai. Rule 222 — Dispose kiye gaye goods ka sale account Form GFR-11 mein banega, jis par sale ya auction ki supervision karne wale officer ke sign honge. Rule 223 — Revaluation, stock-taking ya dusri wajah se hone wale profit/loss record kiya jayega aur zaroorat padne par adjust kiye jayenge. Accounts mein koi correction/adjustment shamil na ho, tab bhi loss ke liye competent authority ki formal sanction leni hogi. Write-off ki powers DFPR ke under hain. Depreciation ki wajah se hone wale loss ke chaar heads hain: market price ka normal fluctuation, normal wear and tear, purchases regulate karne mein foresight ki kami, aur kharid ke baad negligence. Depreciation ke alawa loss ke paanch heads hain: theft ya fraud, neglect, obsolescence ya zaroorat se zyada kharid ki anticipated loss, damage, aur Force Majeure — jaise aag, baadh, ya enemy action. Aakhir mein: abhi ke orders bhi yaad rakhein — DoE ne 10 July 2024 ke OM se disposal threshold ₹2 lakh se ₹4 lakh kiya, aur DARPG ka Special Campaign 6.0 15-30 September 2026 preparation phase, 2-31 October 2026 implementation phase mein chal raha hai, jismein scrap disposal GFR ke hisaab se aur e-waste disposal E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 ke hisaab se karna hai.
