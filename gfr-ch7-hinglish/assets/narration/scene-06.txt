Main transfer ho rahi hoon. Store ka charge aage kaun sambhalega?

Rule 216 — relieving aur relieved officer milkar ek statement banayenge, dono date ke saath sign karenge.

Aur dono ke paas ek-ek copy rahegi — tabhi accountability clear hai.

Rule 216 — Transfer of charge of goods and materials: agar officer-in-charge transfer ho, to transfer hone wale officer ko dekhna hoga ki goods/materials sahi tareeke se uske successor ko hand over diye jayen. Goods aur materials ki sab relevant details ka ek statement banega, jise relieving officer aur relieved officer dono date ke saath sign karenge. Dono officers apne paas signed statement ki ek-ek copy rakhenge. Ye chhota sa rule hai, lekin isi se yah tay hota hai ki agla shortfall kiske account mein jayega.
