Madam, ye A4 paper aur toner pichhle 14 mahine se shelf par pada hai.

Rule 214 ka note yaad rakho — ek saal se zyada stock mein pada material generally surplus maana jaata hai. Buffer stock alag baat hai.

Library alag hai — 20,000 tak volumes ho to har saal poori verification, 50,000 tak 3 saal mein, usse upar sample verification.

Rule 214 — Buffer stock: requirement ki frequency, quantity aur supply pattern ke hisaab se optimum buffer stock competent authority tay karega. Note: inventory carrying cost ek aisa kharch hai jo material ki value nahi badhata — isliye ek saal se zyada time tak stock mein pada material generally surplus maana jayega, jab tak usko alag treat karne ki solid wajah na ho. Aise declared surplus items ko Rule 217 ke procedure se deal kiya jaata hai. Rule 215 — Library books: 20,000 ya usse kam volumes wale library mein har saal complete verification; 20,000 se zyada aur 50,000 tak — kam se kam 3 saal mein ek baar complete verification; 50,000 se zyada — 3 saal se zyada interval par sample verification. Sample verification mein unusual ya unreasonable shortage dikhe to complete verification hogi. Ek saal mein issue/consult ki gayi har 1,000 volumes par 5 volumes ki loss reasonable maani ja sakti hai, agar dishonesty ya negligence ki wajah na ho; lekin ₹1,000 se zyada value ki kitab aur rare books — value chahe jo ho — ki loss ki investigation zaroori hai.
