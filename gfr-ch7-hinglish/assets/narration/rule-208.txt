Rule 208 — Receipt of goods and materials from private suppliers. Yaad rakhne ka formula: C.I.R.T.S. — Contract, Inspect, Record, Technical check, Stock register. Rule 208 mein sirf ek sub-rule hai — Rule 208(1) — jiske andar teen clauses (i), (ii), (iii) hain. Ye tab lagta hai jab maal bahar ke private supplier se aata hai (internal division se aane par Rule 209 lagega). Rule 208(1): Heading clause. Iske niche (i) se (iii) teen mandatory steps hain — contract dekho, verify karo, register karo. Clause (i): Sabse pehla kadam contract terms padhna hai. Delivery schedule, specification, inspection clause, packaging, insurance — jo kuch contract mein likha hai, receipt usi ke hisaab se honi chahiye. Contract ke bina receipt matlab aage chalkar dispute mein department kamzor padega. Clause (ii): Teen physical cheezein har baar honi chahiye: counting, measuring ya weighing, aur visual inspection — aur ye sab receipt ke time par hi. Visual inspection ke teen objective hain: quantity sahi hai, quality specification ke hisaab se hai, aur koi damage/deficiency nahi. Technical inspection "where required" hai — matlab har item par nahi, sirf jahan zaroori ho — aur woh approved Technical Inspector ya approved Agency se hi karwani hogi. Aakhir ka "appropriate receipt... may also be given" ek MAY hai: supplier ko receipt dena contract provisions par depend karta hai. Clause (iii): Do kaam: (1) stock register mein entry — "preferably IT-based", yaani IT system ko tarjeeh hai par ye preference hai, hard mandate nahi; (2) officer-in-charge ka certificate ki usne maal asal mein liya hai aur register mein darj kiya hai. Ye certificate hi receipt ki legal backbone hai. Note. Rule 208 mein koi footnote ya proviso nahi hai — poora rule teen clauses ka hai. Yahan "shall" (counting/measuring/weighing/visual inspection/certificate) aur "may" (receipt to supplier) ka farq exam mein poocha jaata hai. Exception. Technical inspection sirf "where required" — har receipt par technical inspection zaroori nahi. Exam trap: Technical inspection kab karwani hogi? "Where required" — jahan zaroori ho, approved Technical Inspector ya approved Agency se. Har baar nahi. Exam trap: IT-based stock register mandatory hai? Nahi — "preferably in an IT-based system". Par stock register entry aur receipt certificate dono mandatory hain.
