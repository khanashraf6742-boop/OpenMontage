Rule 209 — Receipt / issue of goods and materials from internal divisions of the same organisation.

Yaad rakhne ka formula: Indent → Examine → Acknowledge → (na mile to) entry in indentor copy.

Rule 209 mein chaar clauses hain: (i) internal division se maal mangwana, (ii) stock se maal dena, (iii) contractor ko diye gaye maal ki recovery acknowledgement, aur (iv) poora indent pura na kar sakein to kya karna hai — ye last clause sabse zyada ignore hota hai.

Clause (i): Maal mangwane wala officer (indenting officer) prescribed form mein indent dega. Maal milne par woh khud examine/count/measure/weigh karega — quantity, quality, damage — teen check Rule 208(ii) jaisi hi hain. Farq itna hai ki yahan receipt dena SHALL hai (Rule 208 mein MAY tha): bhejne wale division ko indenting officer likhit/appropriate receipt dega. Isliye internal transfer mein dono taraf record rehta hai.

Clause (ii): Stock se maal nikalne ka rule: Officer-in-charge ensure karega ki prescribed form ka indent pehle hi maujood hai. Uske baad maal issue karte waqt likhit ya online acknowledgement lena hoga — indenting officer ya uske authorised representative se, aur woh bhi issue ke time par. "Written/online" ka matlab hai ki paper register aur digital system dono barabar valid hain.

Clause (iii): Jab maal contractor ko diya jaaye aur uski cost recoverable ho (jaise departmental material contractor ke kaam mein lage), to contractor se sirf "maal mil gaya" nahi likhwana — recovery rates aur total value chargeable bhi likhwayenge, aur woh signed aur dated hoga. Bina iske baad mein recovery dispute mein amount prove karna mushkil ho jaata hai.

Clause (iv): Agar poora indent pura nahi kar sakte (stock kam hai), to itna supply karo jitna uplabdh hai, aur indentor ki copy of indent mein usi ka suitable entry karo — isse indentor ko pata rahega ki kitna baaki hai. Agar indented item ke badle alternative material uplabdh ho to document mein uska zikr kiya ja sakta hai (yahan "may" hai — discretion hai, par note karna acchi practice hai).

Note. Rule 209 ke (i) mein receipt SHALL hai jabki Rule 208(iii) mein supplier ko receipt dena MAY hai — ye contrast yaad rakhiye. (iv) zyadatar departments ke SOP se gayab hota hai — par GFR mein likha hai.

Exam trap: Contractor ko diya gaya maal — acknowledgement mein kya likhwana zaroori hai? Sirf receipt nahi — recovery rates aur total value chargeable, contractor se signed aur dated.

Exam trap: Stock kam ho to indent ka kya karein? Jitna uplabdh hai utna supply karein aur indentor ki copy mein entry karein (Rule 209(iv)).
