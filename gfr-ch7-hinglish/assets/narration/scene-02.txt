Challan kaafi nahi, Ravi. Rule 208 — count, measure ya weigh, visual inspection, phir stock register mein entry.

Aur jab ye samaan doosre division ya contractor ko jayega?

Rule 208 — Private supplier se maal aate hi: contract terms ke hisaab se receipt, phir counting/measuring/weighing + visual inspection (zaroorat par technical inspection), IT-based stock register mein entry, aur officer ka receipt certificate. Rule 209 — internal issue: prescribed form mein indent zaroori + likhit ya online acknowledgement; contractor ko diya gaya maal ho to recovery rate aur total value usse date ke saath sign karwana hoga. Rule 210 — Custody: valuable items aur aag pakadne wale articles ki safe custody, storage ka proper intezaam, temperature control aur dust-free rakhna.
