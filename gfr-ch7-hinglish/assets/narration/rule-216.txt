Rule 216 — Transfer of charge of goods, materials etc.. Yaad rakhne ka formula: Statement, Dono sign with date, Dono ke paas copy. Rule 216 ek paragraph ka rule hai — lekin yahi tay karta hai ki charge badalne ke baad kisi bhi kami ki zimmedari kiski hogi. Rule 216: Teen requirements: (1) jaane wala (transferred) officer ensure karega ki goods/materials sahi tareeke se successor ko hand over ho jayen — sirf chabi dena kaafi nahi; (2) ek statement banega jismein goods/materials ki saari relevant details hon, aur use relieving (charge lene wale) aur relieved (charge chhodne wale) dono officers date ke saath sign karenge; (3) dono ek-ek copy rakhenge. Isi copy se aage chalkar ye tay hota hai ki nayi kami purane officer ki hai ya naye ki. Note. Rule 216 mein koi proviso ya exception nahi — ye har transfer par lagta hai, chahe charge kitne din ka ho. Exam trap: Statement kaun sign karega? Relieving aur relieved — dono, date ke saath. Sirf ek ka sign kaafi nahi. Exam trap: Copy kaun rakhega? Dono officers ek-ek copy rakhenge.
