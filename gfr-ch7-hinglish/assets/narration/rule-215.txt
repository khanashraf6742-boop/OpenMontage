Rule 215 — Physical verification of Library books.

Yaad rakhne ka formula: 20,000 = har saal · 50,000 = 3 saal · usse upar = sample · 5 per 1,000.

Rule 215 library books ke liye Rule 213 ka special exception hai — yahan do clauses hain: (i) verification ka scale-based schedule, (ii) reasonable loss ka norm.

Clause (i): Teen slabs: ≤ 20,000 volumes → har saal complete verification; 20,000 se zyada aur 50,000 tak → kam se kam 3 saal mein ek baar complete verification; 50,000 se zyada → sample verification, jinke beech ka interval 3 saal se zyada na ho. Aakhir ka line sabse important: agar sample verification mein unusual ya unreasonable shortage dikhe to complete verification karni hi padegi. Matlab sample method chhote library ke liye nahi, bade library ke liye hai — aur jab bhi suspicious nateeja aaye, sample se complete par jaana hi hoga.

Clause (ii): Pehla hissa ek concession hai: ek saal mein jitni books issue/consult hui unmein har 1,000 par 5 volumes ki loss reasonable maani ja sakti hai — par sirf tab jab woh dishonesty ya negligence ki wajah se na ho. Doosra hissa ek exception: ₹1,000 se zyada value ki kitaab, aur rare books (value kuch bhi ho) — inki loss ki hamesha investigation hogi. Yani 5/1,000 ka norm sirf ordinary books ke liye hai; mehngi aur rare books ke liye zero tolerance.

Note. Rule 215 Rule 213 ke "at least once in a year" se exception hai — library books par scale-based schedule lagta hai, annual nahi (siwaye ≤20,000 volumes wale library ke).

Proviso. Proviso jaise shabd (i) ke ant mein: "In case such verification reveals unusual or unreasonable shortages, complete verification shall be done." — sample verification ka result hi complete verification ka trigger hai.

Exception. Reasonable loss 5/1,000 tabhi jab dishonesty/negligence na ho. ₹1,000 se upar ki kitaab aur rare books — 5/1,000 ke concession se bahar.

Exam trap: 30,000 volumes wale library mein verification? Complete verification kam se kam 3 saal mein ek baar.

Exam trap: 60,000 volumes wale library mein? Sample verification, jinke intervals 3 saal se zyada na hon.
