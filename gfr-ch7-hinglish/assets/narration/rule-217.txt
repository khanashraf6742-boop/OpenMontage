Rule 217 — Disposal of Goods.

Yaad rakhne ka formula: Declare → Record reasons → Value → GFR-10 → Responsibility → E-waste rules.

Rule 217 paanch clauses ka hai aur poore disposal process ka pehla darwaza hai. Iske bina Rule 218 ka koi mode choose nahi kiya ja sakta.

Clause (i): Declaration tab ho sakti hai jab item Ministry/Department ke kaam ka na rahe. Par declaration kaun karega — woh authority jo item kharidne ke liye competent hai (authority competent to purchase). Aur us authority ko reasons record karne hi honge. "May declare" ka matlab hai declaration discretionary hai; "reasons should be recorded" ka matlab hai ki jab bhi declare karo, likhit wajah zaroori hai.

Clause (ii): Committee banana vivek-adhikar hai — "may, at his discretion". Matlab committee zaroori nahi, par bade ya sensitive lots ke liye banana behtar practice (aur aksar departmental instructions mein likha hota hai). Exam mein ye MAY vs SHALL ka favourite sawal hai.

Clause (iii): Teen prices nikalne honge: book value, guiding price, reserved price. Agar book value nikalna sambhav na ho, to original purchase price use kiya ja sakta hai (yahan "may be utilised" ek permitted fallback hai). Aur disposal ki report Form GFR-10 mein banegi — ye document aage ke saare modes (tender/auction/scrap) ki buniyad hai.

Clause (iv): Agar item kisi Government servant ki negligence, fraud ya mischief se unserviceable hua ho, to responsibility fix karni hogi. Matlab normal wear-and-tear aur insaan ki galti mein farq hai — pehla depreciation hai (Rule 223(2)(ii)), doosra fixable responsibility hai (Rule 223(3)(ii) "losses due to neglect" se judta hai).

Clause (v): Hazardous waste, scrap batteries aur e-waste ki sale Ministry of Environment & Forest (aaj ke context mein MoEF&CC) ke extant guidelines ke under hogi. Ek aur hard condition: aise lots ke prospective bidders ke paas recycler/pre-processor agency ke roop mein registration hona chahiye jo e-Auction ki date par aur delivery ki date par valid ho. Matlab kisi bhi random scrap dealer ko e-waste bechna maana hai — registered recycler hi kharid sakta hai.

Note. Rule 217(v) GFR ko E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 aur MoEF&CC guidelines se jodta hai — isliye Special Campaign mein e-waste disposal ka alag se target hota hai.

Exception. Committee banana discretionary hai (ii) — par reasons, teen prices aur GFR-10 mandatory hain. Book value na nikal sake to original purchase price ka fallback (iii).

Exam trap: Item ko surplus/obsolete/unserviceable kaun declare karta hai? Woh authority jo item kharidne ke liye competent hai (authority competent to purchase), reasons record karke.

Exam trap: Committee banana zaroori hai? Nahi — "may, at his discretion".
