Rule 223 — Write-off of losses.

Yaad rakhne ka formula: Sanction har haal mein · 4 depreciation heads · 5 non-depreciation heads.

Rule 223 teen sub-rules mein hai: (1) powers to write off, (2) depreciation wale losses ke 4 heads, (3) depreciation ke alawa wale losses ke 5 heads.

Clause Rule 223(1): Teen baatein: (1) revaluation, stock-taking ya kisi aur wajah se hone wale profit aur loss dono record kiye jayenge aur zaroorat padne par adjust kiye jayenge; (2) formal sanction competent authority ki leni hogi — chahe government accounts mein koi correction/adjustment ho ya na ho. Ye aakhri line sabse bada exam trap hai; (3) write-off ki powers DFPR ke under hain — GFR khud koi amount nahi batata.

Clause Rule 223(2): Depreciation se hone wale loss ko chaar heads mein baant kar record karna hai: (i) market price ka normal utaar-chadhav; (ii) normal ghisavat (wear & tear); (iii) kharid regulate karne mein foresight ki kami; (iv) kharid ke baad negligence. Dhyan dein — (iii) aur (iv) "normal" nahi hain, ye management failures hain, isliye inhein alag head mein dikhana accountability ka hissa hai.

Clause Rule 223(3): Depreciation ke alawa wale losses ke paanch heads: (i) chori ya fraud; (ii) upeksha (neglect); (iii) obsolescence ya zarurat se zyada kharid ki anticipated losses; (iv) damage (nuksan); (v) Force Majeure — aag, baadh, dushman ki action jaise extraordinary situations. Force Majeure ka head isliye alag hai kyunki wahan kisi ki personal responsibility nahi hoti.

Note. Rule 223(1) ka "even though no formal correction or adjustment in government accounts is involved" — sabse zyada poocha jaane wala line. 4 heads = depreciation · 5 heads = non-depreciation (jismein Force Majeure aakhri hai).

Exception. Force Majeure (aag, baadh, enemy action) — ye head batata hai ki har loss mein kisi ki negligence nahi hoti.

Exam trap: Accounts mein koi adjustment nahi hoga to bhi sanction chahiye? Haan — Rule 223(1) clear roop se kehta hai "even though no formal correction or adjustment in government accounts is involved".

Exam trap: Depreciation wale losses ke kitne heads? 4.
