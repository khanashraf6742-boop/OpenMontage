Rule 214 — Buffer Stock. Yaad rakhne ka formula: Buffer = authority tay kare, 1 saal se upar = generally surplus. Rule 214 ek chhota rule hai par iska Note poore chapter ka sabse cited line hai — "ek saal se zyada stock mein pada material generally surplus". Rule 214: Optimum buffer stock competent authority tay karega — ye koi fixed number nahi jo GFR mein likha ho. Tay karte waqt teen factors dekhne honge: requirement ki frequency, quantity, aur supply ka pattern (lead time, reliability). Matlab buffer stock ek analysis ka nateeja hai, guess nahi. Note. Ye note do kaam karta hai. Pehla: reasoning — inventory carrying cost aisa kharch hai jo material ki value nahi badhata, isliye bina wajah stock rakhna financial propriety ke khilaf hai. Doosra: rebuttable presumption — ek saal se zyada stock mein pada material generally (generally) surplus maana jayega, jab tak use alag treat karne ke adequate reasons maujood na hon. "Generally" aur "unless adequate reasons" dono zaroori hain — ye automatic condemnation nahi hai. Aise declared surplus items aage Rule 217 ke procedure se dispose honge. Exception. "Unless adequate reasons to treat it otherwise exist" — planned/strategic spares, warranty items, seasonal stock jaise reasons record kiye ja sakte hain. Exam trap: Buffer stock ka optimum level kaun tay karta hai? Competent authority — frequency, quantity aur supply pattern ke base par. GFR koi fixed figure nahi deta. Exam trap: 1 saal se zyada stock mein pada material automatic surplus hai? Nahi — "generally" surplus, aur "unless adequate reasons to treat it otherwise exist". Ye rebuttable presumption hai.
