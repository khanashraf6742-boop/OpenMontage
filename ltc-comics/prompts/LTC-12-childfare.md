**Validation (Phase 13):** PASS | Status: CURRENT | Verified: 2026-09-27

"[O.M. No. 31011/3/2016-Estt.(A-IV), dated 16.05.2018] No reimbursement
shall be made in respect of the air journey performed by children, aged less
than 5 years, of the Government servants who are not entitled to travel by
air on LTC." + "[O.M. No. 31011/3/2016-Estt.(A-IV), dated 29.04.2016] actual
