## Rule 14/15 advances: ≤90% of entitled fare; 125-day (rail) / 65-day (other modes) drawal windows; tickets/PNR within 10 days of drawal

**Validation (Phase 13):** PASS | Status: CURRENT | Verified: 2026-09-27
**Anchors (official):** gfr.co.in LTC notes + CGS MCRHRDI presentation +
fnpo DoPT compilation: "Up to 90% of the fare (both outward and return)
can be taken as advance." Windows: train — up to 125 days before; other
modes (road/air/sea) — up to 65 days before. Furnish tickets/PNR to the
Controlling Authority within 10 days of drawal, irrespective of journey
date. Whole outward+return advance only if leave ≤ 90 days; otherwise
outward only; separate advance for family possible.
