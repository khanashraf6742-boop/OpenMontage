## Rule 15 + Compendium on Advances: unutilized advance → refund now; late bill → lump recovery; penal interest PF+2%; claim NOT forfeited

**Validation (Phase 13):** PASS | Status: CURRENT | Verified: 2026-09-27
**Anchor (verbatim, official Compendium on Advances, GID under Rule 2):**
"in case of default, interest @ 2% over the interest rate... on Provident
Fund balances ... (i) advance not utilized fully but adjustment bill
submitted in time: interest on the unutilized portion from date of drawal
to date of refund. (ii) adjustment bill not submitted within prescribed
time: the entire amount of advance may be recovered in one lump sum
immediately on expiry ... interest ... from date of drawal to date of
recovery. The claim of the Government servant for reimbursement would,
however, NOT be forfeited merely on account of recovery of advance. Heads
of Departments may ... waive such recovery or charging of interest."
