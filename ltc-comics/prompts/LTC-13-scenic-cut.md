# COMIC #47 — LTC-13.2 (closer)
## Rule 13 — longer-route/two-class proportionality; circular tour tickets; the add-on charges list

**Validation (Phase 13):** PASS | Status: CURRENT | Verified: 2026-09-26
**Authorities (in-session, official + compilation, verbatim):**
03.09.2025 DoPT Guidelines PDF (CGS publication mirror): "When a Government
servant or any member of his family performs the journey by a longer route
(which is not the cheapest) in two different classes of railway
accommodation, the entitled class rate will be admissible for the
corresponding proportion of the shortest or the cheapest route and the
lower-class rate for the remaining mileage by such route."
itgoawbunit DoPT compilation: circular tour tickets — "the fare at the normal
rate for the entitled/lower class actually used between headquarters and
hometown... by the shortest route... should be reimbursed. Concession shall
not be allowed." Charges list: sleeper charges (II class), reservation
charges (I & II), e-ticketing charges, Service Tax/Edn Cess reimbursable;
agent service charges excluded (Comic #11's verified footer + #6's ATA list).
Math discipline: proportion rule stated as printed, no invented formulas.
